01

What customers say. What customers do.

How attitudinal and behavioural evidence can be combined, and what to investigate when the two disagree.

For: customer insight, CX, product and analytics teams.

02

Why a good insight doesn't get used.

Decision ownership, incentives and the design of the insight function. A discussion about what happens after the read-out.

For: research leaders, senior managers and cross-functional teams.

03

Evidence for learning, or evidence for compliance?

What my doctoral literature review suggests about commissioning and analytical capacity, with the limits of that argument made explicit.

For: researchers, commissioners and third sector leaders.

04

AI in research: what would count as good enough?

Research quality, verification and human judgement. Questions to ask before accepting a faster output as a better answer.

For: insight and analytics teams assessing AI-supported work.

Short biography

Phil Pegram is Head of Insights, Analytics and Market Research at TalkTalk and a DBA researcher at Teesside University. A former Insights Director at dentsu and British Army veteran, he connects customer understanding, analytics and organisational decision-making. His research examines the conditions that allow organisations to use evidence, including commissioning, accountability and analytical capacity.
