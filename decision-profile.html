What this is, and is not

Most online personality quizzes flatter you. Formal selection batteries take the best part of an hour and keep the results. This sits between the two: a short practice diagnostic on how you handle evidence and make commercial calls.

Each attempt draws different questions from a bank, and answer positions are shuffled, so a shared answer key will not work. The numerical and abstract questions are generated afresh each time.

It has not been validated or normed, so there are no percentiles or population comparisons, and it should not be used for hiring or selection. Built by Phil Pegram, who works in customer insight and analytics. Views my own, not my employer's.
