02. About this tool

What it is, and is not

Most online personality quizzes flatter you. Formal selection batteries take the best part of an hour and keep the results. This sits between the two: a short practice diagnostic on how you handle evidence and make commercial calls.

It is a practice and reflection tool. It has not been validated or normed, so there are no percentiles or population comparisons, and it should not be used to make hiring or selection decisions.

  • Verbal and deductive logic (3 scored)
  • Numerical and data fluency (3 scored)
  • Abstract and diagrammatic reasoning (3 scored)
  • Decision style (3 scenarios, no right answers)
