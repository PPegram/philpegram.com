AboutPhil Pegram

The full version

Phil Pegram is a British insights and analytics leader. He is Head of Insights, Analytics and Market Research at TalkTalk, a doctoral researcher at Teesside University, a British Army veteran and founder of Mosaic CIC.

At TalkTalk I lead insights, analytics and market research. The job is to bring attitudinal and behavioural data into one view, close the gap between what customers say and what they actually do, and make sure evidence reaches the decisions that matter across strategy, operations and customer experience.

Before TalkTalk I was Insights Director at dentsu, leading mixed-methods research, analytics, optimisation and UX programmes for clients. Before that I spent 12 years in the British Army as an intelligence analyst, working in geographic and all-source intelligence. That background still shapes how I plan research, weigh evidence and brief people who have to make a call.

I hold an MBA focused on social capital and workforce transformation, completed in 2025. I'm now completing a Doctor of Business Administration at Teesside University, researching evidence-based policy and organisational identity.

Outside work I founded Mosaic CIC (registered as MOSAIC (NW) CIC, company number 16877457), a community interest company researching structural barriers to opportunity in North West England.
